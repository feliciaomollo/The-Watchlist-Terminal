01
Morning Ritual
The Foundation
The 6:30–9:00 AM window is sacred. Phone stays off until 9:00 AM. Twitter is limited to 10 minutes maximum — use a timer. No social media during morning. Every morning begins with hydration, movement, and a written intention for the day.
02
Software Engineering
Deep Work
9:00 AM–12:00 PM is your coding block. Two Pomodoro-style sessions with a 15-min break at 10:30. By month 3: build projects from scratch. By month 6: code confidently without tutorials. No YouTube. No Stack Overflow copying — understand first.
03
Creative Flow
DJ + Production
2:00–6:00 PM is pure creative energy. Alternate daily between DJing and music production. Record every session. By month 2: post your first mix. By month 6: consistent YouTube uploads + a produced EP in the works. Ear rest between sessions is essential.
